# Design Brief: AZ-Europa Service GmbH Redesign

## 1. Project Vision & Objective
*   **Company:** AZ-Europa Service GmbH (Certified Master Enterprise / Meisterbetrieb).
*   **Core Business:** Premium facility management, cleaning, renovations, and gardening.
*   **Service Area:** Franken region, Germany (Erlangen, Nürnberg, Bamberg).
*   **Goal:** Position as the undisputed, most trusted premium provider. Communicate "Sterile cleanliness, absolute trust, and premium corporate German quality."

## 2. Brand Psychology & Visual Identity
*   **Vibe:** Ultra-Clean, High-Trust, Premium Light Theme. Pristine, breathable, medical-grade cleanliness.
*   **Whitespace:** Maximal. High emphasis on "room to breathe."
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Backgrounds:** Pure White (#FFFFFF) and soft, cold grays (#F8FAFC).
    *   **Primary Text:** Deep Navy Blue (#0F172A) and Slate (#334155).
    *   **Accent/Action:** Premium Yellow (#FACC15) for CTAs and highlights.
*   **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (Inter, Roboto, or Outfit). High contrast, large headings.

## 3. Core Architecture
### Homepage (The Conversion Engine)
*   **Header:** Sticky white glassmorphic mega-menu with Yellow "Angebot anfordern" CTA.
*   **Hero:** Bright, professional (no dark overlays). Massive Navy headline. Floating "Trust Card."
*   **Social Proof:** Animated authority matrix (numbers) and grayscale marquee of B2B logos.
*   **Services:** Advanced Bento Grid (asymmetric) with 6 core services.
*   **Interactive Map:** Vector-style map of Franken with pulsing pins for key cities.
*   **Testimonials:** Masonry/Pinterest-style grid with "Verified" green badges.
*   **Lead Form:** Multi-step "Wizard" form (Location -> Service -> Contact).

### City Hub Pages (e.g., /nuernberg)
*   **Aesthetic:** Same clean style but localized.
*   **Features:** Dynamic breadcrumbs, localized Hero imagery (city skyline).

## 4. UI/UX & Mobile
*   **Interactions:** Soft lifts on hover, ripple/shimmer effects on buttons, elegant scroll-triggered fade-ins.
*   **Mobile-First:** 70% of traffic. Full-screen hamburger menu. Sticky "Call Now" and "Get Quote" buttons at the bottom. Bento Grid to stack or carousel.

## 5. Constraints (What to Avoid)
*   NO Dark Mode/Heavy black backgrounds.
*   NO generic/cheap stock photos of people with mops.
*   NO clutter.
*   STRICT 3-color limit (Navy, White/Gray, Yellow).
